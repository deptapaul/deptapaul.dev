Version 1.0.0

Initial Release
November 12, 2026

Welcome to the first release of GreenList! Organize your day, manage your tasks, and build routines with a simple, focused task management experience.

What's included

  • Create and organize tasks using categories.
  • Add task details and subtasks to keep everything clear.
  • Schedule tasks and track completion.
  • Create repeating tasks for regular routines.
  • Customize tasks with background colors.
  • View your tasks and progress with Home Screen widgets.
  • Receive local notifications based on task schedules and repeating-task settings.
  • Use iCloud synchronization to keep supported app data in sync across your devices.
  • Import and export your task data.
  • Unlock additional capabilities with GreenList Premium.

More updates will appear here as new versions of GreenList are released.
