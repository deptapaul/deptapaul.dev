Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

GreenList ("the App") is a task management and productivity application developed by Depta Paul. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use GreenList.

1. Information You Add to GreenList

GreenList allows you to create tasks, categories, descriptions, subtasks, schedules, repeating tasks, and completion records. This information is stored locally on your device using Apple's data storage technologies. We do not operate our own server to collect your task content.

2. iCloud Synchronization

If you enable iCloud synchronization, your GreenList data may be synchronized across your devices using Apple's iCloud and CloudKit services. This data is handled by Apple in accordance with Apple's Privacy Policy. Your use of iCloud is subject to your Apple account settings.

3. Widgets and Task Notifications

If you use the GreenList widget, the App may share the information needed to display your tasks and progress with its widget extension through Apple's App Group storage on your device. GreenList may send local notifications based on task schedules and repeating task settings. These notifications help you keep track of tasks. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

4. Import and Export

GreenList may allow you to import or export your task data. Files you export are saved to a location you choose. You are responsible for protecting and managing those files.

5. In-App Purchases

GreenList offers premium features through Apple's in-app purchase system. Payments are processed by Apple using StoreKit. We do not collect or store your payment card details. Purchase information may be used by the App to verify your premium entitlement.

6. Advertising

GreenList may introduce third-party advertising in a future version. If Google AdMob is integrated, Google and its advertising partners may collect information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraud, and support advertising services. The information collected will depend on the SDK configuration, your region, and your privacy choices.

Any advertising integration will be subject to Google's applicable terms and privacy policy: Google Privacy Policy. We will update this policy and our app privacy disclosures when advertising is introduced.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information or your task content. Information may be processed by Apple services when you use features such as iCloud, StoreKit, and notifications. If advertising is introduced, relevant information may also be processed by the advertising provider as described in this policy.

8. Data Security and Retention

Information stored locally remains on your device until you delete it or remove the App. Information synchronized through iCloud, exported to files, or retained by Apple may remain subject to Apple's storage and retention practices. We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by GreenList, but no method of electronic storage is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

GreenList is a general productivity application and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page with a revised update date. Therefore, we encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about any changes to this Privacy Policy.

11. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact Depta Paul at support@deptapaul.dev.
