Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to help you get the most out of GreenList.

How do I organize my tasks?

You can create custom categories to organize your tasks based on work, personal activities, studies, or any other area of your life. You can also add descriptions, subtasks, and customize task colors.

How do recurring tasks work?

Recurring tasks help you manage activities that repeat on a regular schedule. You can configure recurring options when creating or editing a task.

How can I use GreenList widgets?

Add a GreenList widget to your iPhone Home Screen. You can choose a category to display and view your tasks and progress directly from the widget.

How do I back up my data?

GreenList provides manual backup and export options. You can create a backup and save the backup file to iCloud Drive or another available location using the iOS file interface.

How can I restore my backup?

Open the backup and restore section in GreenList and select your previously exported backup file. Follow the instructions in the app to restore your tasks and categories.

How do I unlock GreenList Premium?

You can unlock Premium through the in-app purchase options available in GreenList. Depending on the available options, you can choose a Lifetime purchase or a Monthly subscription.

How can I restore my Premium purchase?

Open GreenList and use the Restore Purchases option. Make sure you are signed in to the Apple Account used to make the original purchase.

Where is my task data stored?

Your task data is stored locally on your device. Remember to create and safely store backups if you want to restore your data later or move it to another device.

How can I report a problem or suggest a feature?

We welcome your feedback. Send us an email describing the issue or feature you'd like to suggest. Including your device model and iOS version can help us investigate problems.
